About us

Football and community, together

A football and community organisation based in the heart of Leicester, Inner City Club Football (ICCF) has been successfully serving the community since the summer of 2003. Creating football opportunities for young people living in Highfields and neighbouring areas, ICCF aims to unify diversity across Leicester.

ICCF is committed to providing quality coaching and education to build up 5–16-year-olds, with integrity and the ability to play football at a competitive level, through a modern, child-friendly approach to youth football.
